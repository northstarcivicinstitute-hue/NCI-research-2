Workforce transition & skilled pathways

Turn exposure into a verified transition—connecting people to training, credentials, employers and the next step with measures that show whether the pathway actually works.

Four pathway tests

Do not scale a pathway until the basics are verifiable.

Employer validation

Multiple employers recognize the occupation, skills and hiring requirements rather than one sponsor carrying the entire demand claim.

Stackable credentials

Training should connect to recognizable credentials and further education instead of ending in a dead end.

Program readiness

Training length, delivery model, eligibility and financial-aid assumptions are checked against the actual rules that apply.

Placement & value

Completion, placement, earnings, persistence and cost are measured so the pathway can be improved or stopped.

Handoff architecture

Referral is not the outcome.

BuildReady follows the transition far enough to know whether a person actually reached the next institution and remained connected.

01

Validate demand

Confirm the occupation, employer demand, entry requirements and realistic local opportunity.

02

Map the route

Identify training, credentials, aid, transportation, support and institutional responsibilities.

03

Verify the handoff

Track intake, enrollment, placement or another concrete transition instead of counting a referral alone.

04

Measure persistence

Follow early retention, barriers, completion and employer feedback before deciding to expand.

Current Michigan Workforce Pell context

Verified September 28, 2026

Michigan currently identifies basic Workforce Pell program tests that include 150–599 hours and 8–14 weeks of instruction, alignment with high-skill/high-wage/in-demand occupations and multiple employers, stackability into further education, at least one year of operation at the required length and format, and federal performance standards including 70% completion and 70% job placement. The next state application window is January 15–February 12, 2027. Approval is provider- and program-specific; BuildReady itself is not a Workforce Pell-approved program.

Michigan youth-employment handoff

Effective October 2, 2026

Michigan's Youth Employment Permit Portal (YEPP) becomes the required statewide process for youth work permits. Schools will no longer issue new permits after the transition. A previously issued permit remains valid for the current job, but a minor who changes employer or worksite needs a new permit through YEPP; employers hiring minors should register and use the state portal. For BuildReady partnerships involving participants under 18, the permit process belongs in the pathway map rather than being treated as a last-minute administrative step.
