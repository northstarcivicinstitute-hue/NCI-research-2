Sports Policy & Athlete Mobility

MSEEM Forward

Michigan Sports Equity and Economic Mobility is NCI’s framework for examining whether opportunity across the athlete pathway is visible, fair, usable and connected to educational or economic mobility.

Discuss MSEEM ForwardView the athlete mobility continuum

Four practical lenses

What MSEEM Forward examines.

Access

Where cost, geography, participation design and institutional rules shape who can enter or remain in a pathway.

Visibility

How film, data, scouting access and discoverability influence who gets seen and who receives opportunity.

Readiness

Whether schools, athletic departments, athletes and families have usable policies, education and decision support.

Public value

Whether programs and policies produce broader educational and economic opportunity rather than only promotional claims.

Athlete Mobility Continuum

MHSAA → college rules → federal-policy overlay → post-sport mobility.

The continuum gives NCI a single way to track where rules, rights, education and support change as an athlete moves from high school through college and into the next stage of work or education.

1

High school

MHSAA PBA/NIL rules, school boundaries, family decision support and early financial literacy.

→
2

Recruiting & entry

Visibility, offers, enrollment decisions, representation questions and transition into college systems.

→
3

College system

NCAA, conference and institutional rules; NIL contracts; eligibility; transfers; support and athlete protections.

→
4

Federal policy overlay

Pending federal legislation may reallocate or standardize parts of the college-sports rule system. Track proposed changes without treating them as enacted law.

→
5

Graduation & workforce

Credentials, education completion, career transition, financial resilience and whether sports participation expands long-term mobility.

The arrows show a decision sequence, not a hierarchy of legal authority. Applications

Tools connected to the framework.

NIL Access Audit

A structured review of opportunity barriers, recruiting visibility, institutional readiness and athlete-protection questions.

Navigate the Deal

Financial-literacy and NIL/PBA-readiness sessions that translate contracts, taxes, disclosure requirements and exploitation red flags into usable language.

Scorecards and implementation reviews

District- or institution-facing measurement plans that define indicators, data limits, responsibilities and review points.

Youth gambling prevention

A research and implementation concept connecting athlete education, coaches and parents, local delivery channels, privacy-aware measurement and independent evaluation.

Who it is for

Institutions that need a system view.

  • Districts and ISDs assessing sports access and readiness.
  • Athletic departments reviewing NIL/PBA education, recruiting visibility and athlete support.
  • Funders evaluating where resources strengthen durable opportunity.
  • Legislators and agencies seeking observable measures rather than assumptions.
