Independence

How support is handled.

  • Funders do not review or approve findings or analysis.
  • Each brief identifies evidence, NCI analysis and implementation options separately and states its evidence limits.
  • Support for a project does not change what the research concludes.
  • NCI is nonpartisan: it does not endorse or oppose candidates, political parties, legislation or ballot measures.

Northstar Civic Institute

Independent, nonpartisan research on how technology, mobility pathways and sports systems are governed in Michigan.

Northstar Civic Institute is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Vassar, Michigan, serving the Great Lakes Bay Region and the state. NCI is nonpartisan: it does not endorse or oppose candidates, political parties, legislation or ballot measures.
