02Divisions

Two labs and an advisory practice, built for different decisions.

NCI is structured to keep the research lane clean. Each division has a defined function, audience, and operating posture.

i. Community-facing

Community Equity Lab

The Lab translates NCI research into school, district, and community-facing pilots, tools, and implementation support.

Open CEL ii. Institution-facing

Civil Governance Lab

Neutral governance frameworks for surveillance, public records, consumer protection, and emerging public-sector technology. Audits, reporting templates, and implementation tooling for municipalities and the state.

Open CGL iii. Advisory practice

Ledger Strategy Group

LSG provides strategy, policy research support, briefing materials, and implementation planning for civic and public-sector clients on a fee-for-service basis.

03Research lines

Active issue areas across the brief library and policy page.

Six representative lines from the current portfolio. The full set lives across MSEEM, the Civil Governance Lab, and the broader policy page.

a.

ALPR Municipal Data Governance

Surveillance tools, privacy boundaries, procurement discipline, and municipal accountability.

 b.

Municipal FOIA Modernization

Public records systems that are operational, timely, and usable for residents and journalists.

 c.

Auto Lending Transparency

Fairer information and clearer protections for young and first-time vehicle buyers.

 d.

MSEEM Forward

NIL implementation, financial literacy, and athlete mobility pathways for districts and athletic departments.

 e.

Tuscola Literacy and Student Readiness

A county-level readiness framework for K-12 literacy, dyslexia screening, family communication, and movement into CTE.

 f.

GroundWork

Youth agriculture, land-based learning, and workforce exposure through existing Tuscola County infrastructure.
