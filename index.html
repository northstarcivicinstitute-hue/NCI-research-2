Northstar Civic Institute

Research that helps public systems work.

Northstar Civic Institute develops policy research, implementation frameworks and practical tools for Michigan institutions and communities. The work focuses on decisions where authority overlaps, evidence is difficult to interpret or implementation can fail.

How NCI works

Evidence, analysis and recommendation are kept separate.

That distinction makes the work easier to use and harder to overstate.

Map the decision

Identify the actual choice, who controls it and which rules apply.

Test the evidence

State the population, time period, source quality and limits of the available research.

Plan for implementation

Assign responsibilities, define measures, identify risks and explain how results will be reviewed.
