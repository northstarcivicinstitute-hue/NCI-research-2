Northstar Civic Institute

Research that helps public systems work.

Northstar Civic Institute is an independent Michigan research organization focused on how emerging technologies, mobility pathways and sports systems are governed, implemented and measured. NCI clarifies who decides, what the evidence supports and what institutions must do next.

Civic Governance Atlas

Map authority, implementation and accountability.

The Atlas is NCI’s umbrella method for systems where law, contracts, agencies, vendors and institutional rules overlap. It asks who can authorize a decision, what evidence supports it, who controls data and implementation, how performance is evaluated and how a system can be changed or exited.

How NCI works

Evidence, analysis and recommendation are kept separate.

That distinction makes the work easier to use and harder to overstate.

Map the decision
Identify the choice, who controls it and which rules apply.
Test the evidence
State the population, time period, source quality and limits.
Plan for implementation
Assign responsibilities, measures, risks and review points.
