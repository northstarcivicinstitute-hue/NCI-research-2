BuildReady

A pathway architecture, not a generic workforce program.

BuildReady is being redesigned around four tests that partners can verify before scaling a training pathway.

Employer validation

Show that multiple employers recognize the occupation, skills and hiring requirements rather than relying on a single sponsor’s promise.

Stackable credentials

Use industry-recognized credentials that can connect to additional education instead of ending in a training dead end.

Workforce Pell readiness

Where a program seeks Workforce Pell eligibility, design around current state and federal requirements and the provider approval process.

Placement and value

Measure completion, job placement, earnings and whether the pathway delivers value relative to program cost.

Community Equity Lab programs

Two programs, two different handoffs.

Community Equity Lab is NCI’s applied work on the handoffs between schools, training, employers, and services, measured by whether people actually get where they were headed.

GrowReady focuses on earlier readiness, place and exposure. BuildReady focuses on the transition into skilled work, credentials and durable employment.
