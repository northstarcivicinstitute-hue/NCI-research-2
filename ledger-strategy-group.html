Clear separation

Advisory services are distinct from NCI’s public research role.

Northstar Civic Institute publishes independent public research. Community Equity Lab supports applied community work. Ledger Strategy Group provides project-specific strategy and implementation support for clients and partners.

Why the distinction matters

Public research should remain identifiable as public research. Client-directed advisory work has different scope, deliverables and obligations.
