Community Equity Lab

NCI’s community-facing program and pilot platform. The Lab tests whether a pathway works in practice before anyone talks about scale.

What the Lab does

Start small. Verify the handoff. Measure what happens next.

Community Equity Lab translates NCI research into bounded feasibility work, implementation tools and pilots with a defined population, partner roles, measures and decision point.

Frame

Define the population, institution, opportunity and actual implementation problem.

Co-design

Build the workflow with the people who will use, deliver or receive it.

Test

Run a limited pilot with clear safeguards, ownership and referral rules.

Measure

Track reach, handoff completion, burden, persistence and evidence limits.

Decide

Scale, redesign, partner differently or stop based on what the pilot shows.

Partnership posture

Connect what already exists.

The Lab is not built to replace CTE, FFA, 4-H, workforce agencies, employers, schools or community organizations. Its role is to identify where the handoff breaks down, test a practical fix and document whether the fix works.

  • Clear partner roles before launch
  • No implied endorsement or funding
  • Privacy and safety boundaries appropriate to the pilot
  • Measures defined before implementation
  • A stop/redesign decision built into the model

Bring us a real handoff problem

What should happen next, and where does it currently break?

That is the starting question for a Community Equity Lab feasibility sprint or pilot.
