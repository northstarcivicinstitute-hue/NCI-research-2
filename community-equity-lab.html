What the Lab does

The Lab operates where research meets practice, turning NCI's institutional work into tools communities can use.

Design

Pilot programs

We build and run implementation pilots in schools and districts, testing research-backed models before they scale.

Evaluate

Community-centered evaluation

We center lived experience in how programs are measured, ensuring the people closest to the work shape what counts as success.

Translate

Policy guidance

We turn evidence into practical guidance that helps districts, funders, and legislators reduce disparities in student mental health and youth sports access.

Current initiatives

Programs and tools moving through the Lab right now.
