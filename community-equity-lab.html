What the Lab does

Research translated into bounded, testable implementation.

Design pilots

Develop limited projects with clear roles, safeguards, measures, decision points and an explicit plan for what happens after the pilot.

Center user experience

Build participant, practitioner and partner input into definitions, workflow design, implementation and evaluation.

Measure before scaling

Produce practical tools and learning reports that show reach, burden, outcomes, evidence limits and whether expansion is justified.

Operating principle

Pilot + measurement + scale pathway.

A Lab project should begin with a defined population, implementation owner, measurable outcomes, privacy or safety boundaries, and a decision point for continuing, changing, scaling or ending the work.

Start a conversation

Bring a specific implementation problem.

Include the population, institution, decision, available data and timeline. The Lab can then determine whether a pilot, evaluation design or implementation tool is the right fit.
