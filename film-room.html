Michigan Sports Opportunity

Film Room

See the system behind the play

A practical evidence activation that uses cases, film, data and policy walkthroughs to make sports opportunity systems visible and discussable.

See formatsHost a Film Room session

Purpose

Break down the system, not the athlete.

Film Room is not a recruiting or player-evaluation service. It is a structured way to examine how visibility, rules, information and institutional choices affect access to opportunity.

Visibility

What makes opportunity discoverable, and where does access to film, data or evaluation differ?

Rules

What do PBA/NIL, recruiting, eligibility or institutional policies actually require?

Decisions

Where do athletes, families, coaches and institutions need clearer information before acting?

Possible formats

Built to travel.

Case walkthrough

A real policy or pathway case broken into decision points, evidence and institutional roles.

Visibility clinic

A school- or community-facing session on access to film, data, recruiting information and opportunity signals.

Policy film study

A plain-language session on a new rule or governance change and what it does—and does not—change.

Partner roundtable

A structured discussion using common evidence so schools, families, athletes and partners can identify a solvable gap.

Evidence boundary

Useful without overclaiming.

Film Room should distinguish documented rules and evidence from NCI analysis, participant experience and open questions. Sessions should not imply that participation improves recruiting outcomes.

Host a session

Bring one concrete question.

Examples: recruiting visibility, PBA/NIL readiness, athlete harassment, or a new policy that stakeholders need to understand.
