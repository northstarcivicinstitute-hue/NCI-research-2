Sports Policy & Athlete MobilitySept. 28

Senate considers additional amendments to the federal college-sports bill

What happened. The Senate agreed 96-1 to the Baldwin amendment, agreed by voice vote to a Scott amendment on foreign-financing disclosure, and rejected the Paul amendment 1-94. At the evening audit cutoff, the Senate's official roll-call list had not yet posted a final passage vote.

Why it matters. S.4668 remains pending federal legislation. Any final Senate-passed text would still require House action before becoming law.

Civic & Technology GovernanceSept. 24

Michigan joins bipartisan call for federal AI safety testing and state enforcement authority

What happened. Attorney General Dana Nessel joined a 26-attorney-general coalition calling for federal safety testing and standards, transparent government-led incident response and preservation of state enforcement authority.

Why it matters. The request maps directly to the Atlas questions of authority, evaluation, incident response and accountability.

Civic & Technology GovernanceOpen through Oct. 6

NIST seeks comment on AI testing and evaluation framework

What happened. NIST's draft TEVV-Athlon framework is open for comment through October 6 and identifies procurement specialists and business decision-makers among relevant stakeholders.

Why it matters. It creates a narrow opening for NCI to connect technical evaluation to procurement evidence, post-deployment monitoring, human review and exit rules.

Sports Policy & Athlete MobilityOct. 1

MGCB opens another booster-club funding pool

What happened. MGCB says another $100,000 becomes available October 1 for eligible school-affiliated booster clubs, with awards up to $4,000. MGCB also currently lists a GAP Program providing up to $10,000 for Michigan schools and eligible community organizations delivering research-based gambling education.

Why it matters. The best NCI role is independent measurement and implementation support around an eligible local partner, not presenting either pool as an NCI grant.
