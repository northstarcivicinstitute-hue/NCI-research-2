Research posture

One lens across every case: Purpose, Authority, Procurement, Data, Decision, Human Control, Evaluation, Accountability and Exit - supplemented by standard procurement details, exit conditions and a change timeline.

Patterns, not a blacklist. NCI keeps a verified research log of incidents and publishes cross-case patterns, such as which controls fail most often, rather than a running scorecard of agencies or vendors.

Where NCI adds information: Detroit serves as a comparison case when useful, but the focus is on under-studied townships, small cities, the Thumb and the Great Lakes Bay Region, where larger institutions often do not collect local data.

Athlete healthCurrent baseline

The Athletic Trainer Gap

Fact. MHSAA's 2025-26 Sports Medicine Advisory Committee minutes describe an ongoing shortage of athletic trainers in schools, including unfilled grant-funded positions and hiring/retention challenges. Michigan's concussion law separately requires recurring training, immediate removal for suspected concussion and written clearance before return to activity.

NCI watch. Track school-level athletic-trainer access, hours of coverage, referral time, rural and small-school differences, and staffing models. Use current MHSAA evidence alongside peer-reviewed Michigan access research; do not treat concussion-law compliance as a proxy for actual clinical coverage.

Original brief →MHSAA advisory minutes →Michigan concussion law →Michigan access study (PubMed) →
NIL readiness2026 rules in force

NIL/PBA Financial Literacy for Student-Athletes

Fact. MHSAA expanded its Personal Branding Activity rules effective January 27, 2026. Michigan high-school student-athletes may receive compensation for individual PBA opportunities under specific restrictions, including limits on school identifiers, pay-for-play and certain prohibited product categories.

NCI watch. Keep high-school PBA education separate from college NIL and revenue-sharing rules. Financial-literacy materials should also address tax basics: the IRS states that NIL compensation, including certain noncash compensation, is generally taxable and must be reported.

Consumer financeSept. 18

Auto Lending Transparency and First-Time Buyer Protections

Fact. Michigan joined a $694 million multistate settlement with Credit Acceptance Corporation resolving allegations involving unaffordable subprime auto loans and dealer packing of vehicle-service contracts and GAP products. Michigan's attorney general says approximately $70.3 million in debt forgiveness is allocated to Michigan consumers; the settlement is scheduled to take effect November 2, 2026.

NCI watch. Use the settlement as a concrete enforcement example for affordability, add-on consent, dealer incentives, disclosure design and lender oversight. Keep allegations, settlement terms and independent NCI analysis clearly separated.

PlayStrong Research and Implementation Brief

Fact. Michigan's Profile for Healthy Youth (MiPHY) is not available for the 2026-27 school year. MiPHY normally provides local and regional student-health measures across risk behaviors, emotional health and protective factors.

Municipal governanceSept. 24-29

Automatic License Plate Readers and Municipal Data Governance

Fact. On September 24, Michigan House members introduced two ALPR-related legislative tracks: HB 6411-6413 address regulation, penalties and sentencing provisions, while HB 6346 addresses sharing of captured plate data. The measures were referred to House Judiciary. Separately, Lansing City Council adopted a 7-1 resolution asking the administration and police to terminate Flock contracts, and Eastpointe voted 4-1 to end its contract after police substantiated misuse by two employees.

NCI watch. Track the bill texts and municipal cases separately. The recurring governance questions are authorized use, audit logs, retention, interagency sharing, discipline, procurement terms, and whether local governments have practical suspension and exit rights.

Sports literacy & wellbeing2026 evidence

Sports Literacy and Mental Health Survey

Fact. NCAA's 2026 SNAP research found substantial betting-related and social-media abuse among Division I basketball athletes; separate 2026 GOALS and SNAP findings report generally strong career confidence while showing that transition out of competitive sport remains a distinct support issue.

NCI watch. A Michigan survey should measure sports knowledge, betting exposure, harassment, help-seeking, mental wellbeing and transition readiness as distinct constructs. Preserve denominators and population limits so college-athlete findings are not presented as directly representative of Michigan high-school athletes.
