Module families

Decision-readiness across the pathway.

NIL / PBA readiness

Contracts, school rules, disclosure, taxes, representation, prohibited categories and exploitation red flags.

Recruiting decisions

Offers, visibility, fit questions, costs, promises and the difference between interest and a verified opportunity.

Transfer & eligibility

Questions to verify before making a move that could affect participation, enrollment or support.

Betting & prediction-market literacy

Age restrictions, integrity issues, harassment risks, prohibited activity and help resources.

Post-sport mobility

Education completion, credentials, career preparation, financial resilience and the transition into work.

Delivery principle

Education, not individualized legal or financial advice.

Next Play can provide plain-language education and decision frameworks. Athletes and families should use qualified legal, tax, financial or compliance professionals when a decision requires individualized advice.

Potential hosts

Schools, clubs, athletic departments and community partners

Sessions should be tailored to the age group, ruleset and actual decisions participants face.
